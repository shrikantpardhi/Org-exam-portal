# Master PRD v1.0

## 05. Functional Requirements

## 5.1 Identity and Access Management

- The platform shall support secure authentication using email/mobile and password.
- The platform shall enforce role-based access control.
- The platform shall support login sessions, logout, and password reset.
- The platform shall support organization-wide user management.

## 5.2 Academic Structure

- The platform shall support Exam → Subject → Chapter → Topic → Sub Topic hierarchy.
- The platform shall support languages and tags as reusable masters.

## 5.3 Question Bank

- The platform shall support reusable question templates.
- The platform shall support versioned questions.
- The platform shall support assets, explanations, hints, and previous exam history.
- The platform shall support review, approval, and publishing workflows.

## 5.4 Blueprint and Assessment Management

- The platform shall support versioned blueprints.
- The platform shall support manual, rule-based, and hybrid assessment creation.
- The platform shall support assessment versioning, validation, preview, and publishing.
- The platform shall support assessment collections and scheduling.

## 5.5 Assessment Delivery

- The platform shall support secure exam sessions.
- The platform shall support timers, navigation rules, question palette, and auto-save.
- The platform shall support session recovery and response synchronization.

## 5.6 Evaluation and Results

- The platform shall support automatic evaluation using answer keys.
- The platform shall support scoring, rank, percentile, and scorecards.
- The platform shall support re-evaluation and result publication workflows.

## 5.7 Analytics

- The platform shall support student, assessment, question, topic, and organization analytics.
- The platform shall support performance trends and recommendations.

## 5.8 Commerce

- The platform shall support collections, access packages, subscription plans, purchases, payment records, coupons, and access grants.

## 5.9 Communication

- The platform shall support notifications, email templates, and announcements.

## 5.10 Administration

- The platform shall support organization settings, business rules, lookup values, audit logs, and operational controls.

## Status

Frozen architecture baseline for Version 1.0.
