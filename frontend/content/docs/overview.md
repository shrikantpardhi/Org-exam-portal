# Smart Competitive Examination Platform

The platform is a modular examination system that takes an institute from question authoring to
published results. It provides a configurable exam engine, a question bank with LaTeX rendering,
three examination patterns (NTA, TCS iON, and IBPS-style), analytics, commerce, communication, and
reporting.

## Getting around

Sign in through one of three portals:

- **Student** — attempt exams and view results, scorecards, and reports.
- **Organization admin** — manage an organization's users, content, schedules, and configuration.
- **System admin** — manage organizations, providers, and the platform.

Every role lands in its own home, and every capability is permission-gated so users only see what
they are allowed to do.
