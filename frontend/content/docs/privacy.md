# Privacy policy

The platform processes personal data only to operate the examination service.

- Accounts store the name and email address needed to authenticate and contact you.
- Examination attempts and results are retained as long as the organization requires them.
- Audit events record who changed what, for accountability and security.
- Personal data is not sold or shared with third parties except as required by law.

For requests about your data, contact the support channel listed on the support page.
